# Animal Stations (game 7): read this first

Egg animal collector, at framework stage: hatch animals, level their six stats at your ranch, and
send a team of three plus a Titan through 300 waves. The ranch is the player's base (a tycoon
later); the hub is for vendors. Keep things simple: placeholders, draft numbers.

- Project `stations.project.json` (CI builds and place-checks it). No publish environment yet.
- Docs: `docs/game-design/game7-gdd.md` (the current summary), `docs/game-design/game7-animals.md`
  (roster and looks), decisions 0019 to 0046 (0029 to 0046 are the latest and replace parts of the rest).
- Not built: wave battles in the arena (so coins and samples cannot be earned in play), what waves pay. In Studio only, the ranch menu has a test funds button. Gear is not designed: the gear vendor only has a message.

Pure rules in `src/shared`: `StationConfig` (stats, stat levels, roles, groups, species, variants,
eggs, stations), `StationRules` (hatch, stat levels, roles), `BattleRules` (wave fights on a clock),
`LevelRules` (waves, rewards), `StationProgress` (saved profile and every change to it), `ValleyMap` (the shape of a ranch's valley: display only, no game rule reads it). Each has a spec.

## Must stay true

- Robux (decision 0117): `MarketplaceService.ProcessReceipt` is in `StationsServer` and nowhere else. A purchase is granted by `StationProgress.grant`, idempotent by the receipt's PurchaseId (`Profile.receipts`), and reported `PurchaseGranted` only after `saves.flush` stored it; anything else returns `NotProcessedYet`. What is sold is `StationConfig.PRODUCTS` (a `product` id of nil is not on sale). The beta reward is `StationConfig.BETA_GIFT`, given once (`Profile.beta`) at load.
- Analytics (decision 0113): `Analytics.luau` logs the funnel, every coin and sample change (typed by `Analytics.because`, set by every request and each payout) and three custom events; never clicks or presses. A new payout should call `Analytics.because` before its `saves.apply`.
- Release and fusion (decision 0112): `StationProgress.release` and `fuse` (four of one animal and coat tier into the next tier, `COAT_TIERS`); never on an animal in the team, training or locked. Trading is off while `StationTrade.ON` is false. Training remembers the team `slot` it left and `stop` puts it back.
- Saves (decision 0111): a busy save is waited for behind the loading cover (`waitBusy`), a session that cannot save kicks the player, and a save from newer content is never loaded (`tooNew`): bump `StationProgress.VERSION` with every release that adds species, variants, eggs or coats. A run's pot is banked in `beforeFinish`.
- The server decides prices, hatches and times; profile changes are pure `StationProgress` functions that capture their inputs (SaveStore runs them twice).
- "Level" has three meanings: wave (`LevelRules`, `Profile.best`), stat level (0 to `MAX_LEVEL`, `Owned.trained`) and player level (`StationProgress.playerLevel`, from `Profile.xp`).
- `BattleRules` is for wave fights only. Fights between players will work differently and stats will not affect them: build nothing with PvP in mind.
- Each stat has exactly one job in a wave fight (Speed swing, Stamina rest, Strength damage, Mana energy per attack, Health HP, Defence damage cut); the spec checks it. A stat's value is the species number grown by its level, so species still matter.
- Training runs in real time, also while the player is away, with no cap; level times come from `StationRules.levelSeconds` (whole seconds). A station trains one animal at a time (decision 0066): `StationProgress.train` refuses a second with `station_busy`, and `sanitize` frees a second one a stored save holds.
- The animal that trains is seen in its station's area (decision 0066): `Areas.act` says where it stands and which clip it plays for a station and Training Grounds level (`WORK`, in the area's own numbers), `syncTrainers` in `StationsServer` stands it there and moves the two that travel (the track's lap, the pool's length), and it is out of the roaming herd while it trains. The six clips are `TRAIN` in `Creatures.client` (run, heave, swim, study, soak, brace): looping pose functions like the walk, with a branch for upright animals; a head's first number is its pitch and up is positive. A new station needs a `WORK` entry and a clip.
- A role and a group come from the species, never from stats. The Titan is never in `Profile.team`; it uses `Profile.titan`.
- `sanitize` repairs an animal's missing stats from its species; it only drops unknown species and duplicate ids. Keep it that way when stats change.
- World and Ranch are display only. Tags for the server: `TrainPad` (`Ranch`, `Station`), `RanchSpot` (`Ranch`, `Index`), `RanchNest` (`Ranch`), prompts `RanchMenu`, `EggStorage`, `Egg` (`Index`, `Due`) and `Vendor` (`Kind`), all with `Ranch`, `Teleport` prompts (`To` = "hub" or "ranch"), `FieldStand` and `FieldGate` on the battlefield. Everything on a ranch is built by Ranch.luau and replaced when the ranch or its eggs change: reach them through CollectionService signals, never keep a reference.
- Eggs (decision 0036): the animal is rolled at purchase and kept in the save; the client only ever gets `StationProgress.public`. Nothing starts hatching or opens by itself: `start` and `crack` are asked for by the player. Prompts act on the client (`ProximityPromptService.PromptTriggered` in the HUD), which checks the ranch is the player's own; a prompt must stay enabled on the server or its trigger is dropped.
- Ranch upgrades (decisions 0034, 0072) are data in `StationConfig.UPGRADES`; `StationProgress.value` is the only place their effect is read. A save is never cut down to fit an upgrade level: `MAX_EGGS` and `MAX_STORED` are the most a save can hold (the top levels of the Hatchery and the Egg Cellar), while `start` and `canBuy` ask the levels. An egg's time is `StationProgress.hatchSeconds` (the Brooder), never `egg.hatch` alone. The Windmill fills from the save's `mill` clock and pays only when its owner collects (decision 0073): `StationProgress.held` says what it holds, `collect` pays it, and `settle` must not. The till at the mill carries a prompt tagged "Mill"; the owner's client writes the sum on its board. The Trophy Lodge is in `payout`. The Titan's Den puts `boost` on the copy of the Titan that `fighters` returns, which `StationRules.stat` reads: it is never stored.
- A homestead is open (decision 0072): no fence round it (only the team's pen round the meadow, decision 0080), no lawn of its own (`World.LAWN` is 0: a part's grass shows far darker than terrain). Ranch.luau places things by polar numbers in the ranch's plan (studs from the middle, degrees where 90 is away from the hub: ValleyMap's): the meadow (radius 34), the ring path (to `ValleyMap.RING`), each building at `PLACE`, the rest between. A building is the model `home_<upgrade id>_<look>` (`lookOf` spreads its levels over three looks; 0 is a staked plot), stood like an area's model with its front to the middle, plus unseen `Solid` blocks and what the game adds (the door's prompt, nests, the egg rack, sails). A building's own numbers are those of `tools/blender/stations/scenes/homestead.py`: change the two together. A building's right, seen from the middle looking out, is towards the bigger angles; put what stands beside one on the side that is free. Scattered trees and bushes keep off `PLACE`, `FIXED` and the ways: add a new fixed thing to `FIXED`.
- A mountain's close-up detail (foothills, pines, ledges) is built only on the side given as `toward`. Sign text uses `Props.label`'s inset so posts never cover it.
- The Titan's Den is dug into a hill (decision 0075): `ValleyMap.DEN` says where; `ValleyMap.height` raises the hill and `Valley.den` cuts its hall out of the terrain after it is written. Its model is only the gate and what is inside; the hall's walls are the hill's rock.
- The Titan in its den sleeps (decision 0079): the server plays `sleep`, a `TRAIN` pose in `Creatures.client`, which also lays lids over its eyes from `StationAssets`' `eyes` and `lid` (recorded by `build.py` from pieces named "eye" and "brow") and puts a "Zzz" in the player's gui (a BillboardGui in the model did not show).
- The look (decision 0085): lighting in `World.luau`, terrain colours in `Valley.colours`, grass blades on the place (`stations.project.json`: a script may not set `Terrain.Decoration`). Blades grow up through whatever lies flat on Grass: lay LeafyGrass under a new floor, pond or road (`calm`). Trees, pines and bushes are the `scenery_*` models, stood by `kit.tree` / `kit.pine` / `kit.bush` without waiting, with the old parts as the fallback.
- Round each training ground (decision 0086): its floor frays into the land (`ValleyMap.kind`, not the pool's), and `Valley.dress` stands a border on its rim and woods up the slopes, kept off paths, water, other grounds and the den's hill (`wild` in Valley.luau). The track's floor is LeafyGrass so no blades grow through it. On the rock round them (decision 0087), `rocky` finds spots on a fixed grid: grassy tops (`Valley.tops`, called after `Valley.write`) where it is gentle, ledges set into the face where it is steep. The whole homestead is LeafyGrass (`calm` in World.luau), so nothing there has blades through it.
- `Ranch.render` must never wait: stand a model with `task.spawn(Creatures.still, ...)`, as the areas do. If it waits (a model's first load does), an upgrade bought meanwhile starts a second rebuild beside the first and the ranch stands twice over.
- Ranch.luau builds from the upgrade levels and a fixed seed, so the same levels always give the same ranch, and a higher level only adds to the scattered scenery. Keep creatures and anything else that must survive an upgrade out of the plot's `Built` folder. A ranch's `Owner` attribute is the UserId or 0.
- Ranches lie behind the hub's mountains (`World.RANCH_DIST`, 900: less and one ranch's valley meets the next's), south, east and west; the north is the battlefield. Each is reached by teleport or on foot up its pass (`pass` in World.luau: a road, mountains and walls down both sides; the slope is the valley's own land). Smooth terrain stands 2 studs above what a column is filled to, and is only exactly level on a 4-stud step: the homestead is at `World.RANCH_Y` = 22 and every ground's height is a multiple of 4 (a spec checks); the hub block tops at -2 for a surface at 0.
- A ranch is a valley (decision 0070). Its shape is `src/shared/ValleyMap.luau`, in the ranch's own plan (x to its right, z away from the hub, heights above the homestead): `height`, `water`, `kind`, the grounds (`GROUNDS`, in `StationConfig.STATIONS`' order), the paths (`WAYS`), the stream. `Valley.write` puts it in the terrain and `Valley.dress` adds parts. Move a ground, a path or the road only there, then run its spec: it checks that every ground is level and clear of the others and that every path can be walked. All three ranches have the same valley. A ranch must be turned a whole number of quarter turns (`RANCH_ANGLE`), since voxels lie along the world's axes.
- Every area is closed: mountains (rock domes) with an invisible `Barrier` wall in front. The hub's wall is at `World.HUB_RADIUS` and opens only onto the road north (walled up to and round the battlefield) and the three passes; keep new hub content inside it.
- A variant is a drop of its own (decision 0056): an egg lists its variants in `rare` (species, variant, ppm), `StationRules.hatch` rolls them first, and a variant that no egg lists never hatches (a spec checks every variant is listed, except the exclusive ones: decision 0117). Eggs unlock by player `level` (`StationProgress.playerLevel`; the Meadow and Wild Eggs have none), not the best wave (SHANNON, 2026-11-24). Every animal has two egg variants (decisions 0050 to 0053, 0110); the Lion and the Elephant also have an exclusive one of the Eternal rarity, never from an egg (decision 0117). The lions, elephants, jackals and cheetahs are faceted, built in `tools/blender/stations/assets/lions.py`, `elephants.py`, `jackals.py` and `cheetahs.py` on the helpers in `tools/blender/stations/facet.py`, apart from the shared predator body. A faceted model must be exported without vertex colours (`kit.py` drops them after the bake): Roblox multiplies the texture by them and the model comes out dark.
- A hatched animal's `stats` are exactly its version's numbers; what varies is `trained`, the stat levels (base 1 to `BASE_MAX` on hatch, jackpot `MAX_LEVEL`).
- Places are built from Blender models too (decisions 0064 and 0065): the hub's plaza is eight models `hub_*` (`tools/blender/stations/assets/hub.py`, from the makers in `scenes/hub_commons.py`), a training area the models `area_<station>_<first>_<last>` (`assets/areas.py`, from `scenes/training.py`), each holding what stands from Training Grounds level `first` to `last`. Such a model is built where it stands and exported uncentred (`finalize: center False`); the manifest records its `lift` and `Creatures.build` puts its Root on the place's ground, so `Creatures.still(name, frame * CFrame.Angles(0, math.pi, 0), 1, parent)` stands it with the place's axes. It is one painted `body` and a Neon piece for each glow colour (`stage.sort_pieces`). These models take no collisions: `plaza` in `World.luau` and `SOLID` in `Areas.luau` add unseen `Solid` parts, their places read off the scene files in the scenes' own numbers; change the two together. `Creatures.publishPortraits` skips `hub_` and `area_` models. The plaza's animals are plain `Creatures.spawn` models that only idle.
- A ranch's six training areas stand on the valley's grounds: `World.areaFrame(i, k)` (half a stud under the ground's land, looking away from the homestead; an area's gateway is on its back) and `World.AREA_GATE` (where its path leaves the homestead's lawn: its name board stands there) both come from `ValleyMap.GROUNDS`. An area has no floor or fence of its own (`OPEN` in `scenes/training.py`): the land is its floor, so `SOLID` in `Areas.luau` holds only what stands above it. `Areas.build` stands the models whose stretch holds the Training Grounds level and is called by `Ranch.render`, so an upgrade rebuilds them. All six follow the one upgrade.
- The training kit moves (decision 0067), on each client, in `stations-client/Kit.client.luau`. A piece that moves is a model of its own (`kit_<station>_<piece>`, built by `assets/areas.py` and left out of the area's models: `KIT` in `scenes/training.py`), a whole area model (the boulder, the straw men) or one Neon piece of one (`MOVES` in `Areas.luau`, by its colour). `Areas.build` stands and tags it ("Kit", attribute `Kit` = how it moves, `Axis` = how high its middle rests: a piece's meshes reach a client after its tag, so the client must not measure them when it is added). `StationsServer` marks each station's folder with what its animal works at (`Work`, the `kit` of its `WORK` entry) and the animal (`Worker`, an ObjectValue). The bar and the ram are put where that animal's back and head are drawn each frame, so they fit any animal. An animal's clip and its kit share `src/shared/TrainFx.luau`'s curves, which is what keeps them in time: change a lift's or a blow's timing there, never in one script alone. Never turn anything by the server's clock directly (too large a number for a CFrame): add up an angle.
- Training is fitted to an animal's size (decision 0069): `Areas.class` gives 1, 2 or 3 from its model's height, a `WORK` entry may give a place and a piece for each class (`sized`, `kits`), the gym has boulders and barbells of three sizes (`COPIES`: the same models stood smaller), and `Areas.fit` sizes the ram's log to the animal that arrives and gives a small reader a book. A piece answers to a name (`Answers`) and the station's `Work` names the one in use. A new animal needs nothing; a new station should say what each class does.
- Mountains go wherever they keep out of the `zones` of `World.luau` (the hub, the arena, every valley as far as its unseen wall, and every pass): place a new one with `seated`, which shrinks it to fit or leaves it out, never with `mountain` directly, and add a zone for anything new that must stay clear. Ring a new closed space with `wallRound`.
- The SpawnLocation is placed in code (`World.SPAWN`, the hub fallback); Rojo ignores `Position` in the project file. Owners are moved to their ranch after spawning.
- The enemies of the waves are the Hexbound (decision 0040), `StationConfig.FOES`, never animals: a foe is not in `SPECIES`, never hatched, owned or saved (`sanitize` and `hatch` look only at species). `StationConfig.foe` and the `StationRules.role` and `BattleRules.fighter` lookups are the only places that know both. Only `LevelRules.enemies` builds them.
- Eggs are models too (decision 0057): assets `egg_<id>`, one painted `body` and an optional Neon `body_glow`, no joints. An asset with no head is a still prop: `Creatures.build` leaves its pieces anchored round a Root and `Creatures.still` stands a copy at a scale. Stand an egg in the world with `World.kit.eggModel` (it returns an unseen part for a prompt or a light at once; the model loads behind it, with the plain shell as the fallback). The menus' egg pictures and the hatching animation use the same still copies as the portraits.
- Coats (decisions 0058, 0110): `StationConfig.COATS` (Prismatic +30% every stat, Spectral, Galaxy, Gilded +25% XP, Rainbow +5%, five +12% stat coats), one at most per animal, rolled after the animal in `StationRules.hatch` (`pickCoat`: an egg's signature `coat` `COAT_TIMES` as likely; `always` eggs scaled so every hatch has one). An animal stores `coat = id`, and `rainbow = true` beside `coat = "rainbow"`; a save with only `rainbow = true` reads as the rainbow coat (`StationRules.coatOf`): never rewrite saves for coats. `StationRules.stat` adds the buff, so read a stat through it, never from `stats` directly; Spectral and Galaxy are in `BattleRules.fighter`/`hurt`, Gilded in `StationProgress.payout`. Every animal and variant needs a rainbow texture: after building and exporting a new one, run `tools/blender/stations/rainbow.py` and `export_assets.py --rainbow`, and upload `<asset>_rainbow` with it (a spec checks). `Creatures.spawn(..., coat)` puts the rainbow texture on Rainbow and Prismatic and `COAT_LOOKS` (a Highlight and an effect) on the rest; `Creatures.describe` sets `Rainbow` and `CoatId`; `Portrait` takes `rainbow` in its options.
- Rarity by power (decision 0110): `StationRules.powerRarity` gives the band a version's stat total falls in (`RARITY_FLOOR`; Titans `TITAN_BAND` wider) and a spec checks that every species' and variant's rarity is the one its power earns. A variant's numbers are its species' x its own `mult` (+ `TWIST_PER` x mult on its twist stat): change a `mult` and its `tier` together. Twelve eggs (`EGGS`): the first seven drop animals and rare variants, the last five (`only`) nothing but variants, their drops adding up to a million. The eight new eggs' models are in `tools/blender/stations/assets/eggs.py` (uploaded, upload-assets run 45377796460); a crown or horn may stand an egg up to 4.5 studs tall.
- A foe's model, size and tint are in its `StationConfig.FOES` entry; `Creatures.spawnFoe` and `Creatures.foeSize` read them. Models for two-legged foes carry a `Biped` attribute (the client uses the biped poses), the dragon `Winged`; set in `Creatures.build` from the asset name, so a new foe model named `foe_<kind>` needs a kind added to `FOE_KINDS`, a pose set if it is not four-legged, and an entry in the foe tests.
- Real wave fights (decision 0043): the `wave` action in `StationsServer` decides with `LevelRules.fight`, pays with `StationProgress.clear`, then `WaveShow.run` replays it; one fight in the arena at a time (`WaveShow.active`), and `readyAt` keeps a player from starting the next one before this one's fight time is up. `WaveShow.run` calls its `report` exactly once (at the end or when cleared). The wave gate is a prompt tagged `WaveGate` (World.luau); the client opens the wave list.
- The HUD (decision 0044) is `stations-client/Hud.client.luau` over `Style.luau` (colours, cards, chips, buttons, sheets) and `Portrait.luau` (a picture frame of a model; the server publishes the models in `ReplicatedStorage.Portraits`). Since STATION-135 it is one script cut into files: `Hud.client.luau` builds the frame, the player card, the dock and the empty sheets, loads the parts in order (`HudAnimals`, `HudRanch`, `HudEggs`, `HudGifts`, `HudTrade`, `HudWaves`, `HudFight`, `HudWaveMenu`, `HudHover`, `HudHatch`, `HudIntro`, `HudMoments`: each fills its sheets when required and returns nothing), then listens to the server (the Refreshing block). What the parts share goes through `HudApp`: a part sets `App.name = name` once at its end and a later part takes it as a local at its top (asking for a name nobody has set is an error at load). A shared name that is assigned again later is never copied: it lives on `App.S` and is read and written as `S.name` everywhere (`profile`, `openName`, `ranchTab`, `wave`, `tradeState`, `invite`, `tradePeek`, and the functions set by a later part: `hideResult`, `openWaves`, `moveName`, `fightTick`, `music`). A new screen is a new `Hud*` module added to the list in `Hud.client.luau`. A chunk may hold 200 locals at once (Luau's limit): the main script has room for 133 more and `HudFight`, the tightest part, for 124 (it was 28 for the whole HUD after STATION-134). Only locals a later function uses count (Roblox's compiler drops number constants and locals no function captures once they are past their last use). To measure the room, in Studio's Edit mode `loadstring` a script's `Source` with N used dummy locals in front (tables, not numbers); Lune's older compiler counts about 20 to 30 more, so it warns early. Lay everything out in design units inside the scaled root; make buttons at least 44 units tall; give cards a fixed size and let them wrap. Set a player's `UiSize` (Vector2) and `UiOpen` (a sheet's name) attributes to look at a phone or tablet layout on a desktop, and `UiInput` ("touch" or "keys") to stand in for the device's input.
- The look is Sun and Moon (decision 0114, plan and state in `docs/checkpoints/STATION-LOOK.md`): paper and ink at the ranch (`Style.C`, `Style.pill`, `Style.card`), the same shapes in dark glass in a fight (`Style.N`, `Style.glass`), where gold only ever means "press this now". Icons come from `Style.icon` (the studio's outlined set, no uploads), a key from `Style.key` (it hides itself on a touch screen), small print from `Style.small`. The ranch HUD is built in `Hud.client.luau`: the level ring and purses in the root; the dock (keys 1 to 5), the goal strip, To battle (B), the timers and the top-right notes in `nav`, which hides for an open menu and for a fight (`S.fighting`). Each of those pieces is a `do` block that leaves one `Hud.` function behind (`Hud.showGoal`, `Hud.waiting`, `Hud.eggReady`, `Hud.nextWave`).
- The menus (STATION-140, the concept boards Animals, Eggs, Waves, RanchGifts): `Style.sheet` is the boards' window, a header band with the menu's name, `chips` (purses, limits) and `count` (what can be done now, on the right) over the body; a menu that fills `tabs` gets a tab column. A menu in columns (Animals, Eggs, Waves) draws on `Style.page(sheet)` (the body's place, not scrolling) with one `Style.column` each, which scrolls on its own. The vendor is the Eggs window's shelf: `show("shop")` opens Eggs. Ranch (900 wide) and Gifts (820) are one page each. Nothing opens a menu by itself (SHANNON, 2026-11-24), and eggs open one at a time (`Hud.openEgg` queues by nest until the last reveal is over).
- Moments (STATION-141): every message goes through `Style.toast`, a stack of up to four top right, glass while the screen's `Night` attribute is set (HudFight sets it for a fight). `HudMoments` draws the server strip (`Hud.announce`), the level card (`Hud.levelUp`, from the profile handler, held until a fight is over) and the line under a hatch's reveal (`Hud.hatchChoice`); `Hud.chooseAnimal` and `Hud.pickEgg` open Animals and Eggs on one thing.
- Goals (decision 0115): `Shared.StationGoals` is the ordered list; the save keeps the ids done (`goals`); the server pays whatever the save shows as reached after every change (`payGoals`, from `onChanged`) and sends `goal`. The HUD only draws `StationGoals.next`.
- The intro (decision 0116): `Shared.StationIntro` says who the Ranger lends, on which wave and with what setup (`immortal` in `BattleRules.Setup`: the team cannot lose its last point of health), and what finishing gives (three `quick` starter eggs, already hatching). The server's `intro` request starts the borrowed fight as a show (`WaveShow.run`, no pay, no wave opened) or skips it; the save's `intro` is set once. `HudIntro.luau` draws the offer, the four lessons (it reads `S.strikes` and `S.fired`, which `HudFight` fills) and the goodbye; while `S.intro` is set the ranch HUD stays hidden (`Hud.navShown`).

Each piece of the ranch HUD adds a function to `layouts` in `Hud.client.luau` that places it for a desk or a phone (`Hud.relayout`, run when the root's size or the input changes); on a phone the dock is a square of four above jump and Ranch and Travel are in More. An animal whose front-leg joints are more than twice as high as its hind ones stands upright (the meerkats, decision 0055): `Creatures.build` scales it by its hips and gives it `Biped`, so it stands, walks and falls like a two-legged Hexbound but keeps its own attack clips. `Creatures.client` lifts a body by however far a pose pushes a leg's box under the ground it stands on (not in a fall), so a clip need not be fitted to every animal's legs; a skinned animal (bones, no boxes) is lifted from the foot points `src/shared/AnimalRig.luau` measured on its model, in its idle, attack, power attack and flinch only (its training clips and the Titan's sleep were approved without); an upright animal's tail never swings below where it rests. Move a walking creature at `Creatures.speed` or its feet slide.

The herd at the end of the server script shows the owner's team wandering the team's pen in the meadow (`Ranch.somewhere`; decision 0080: every other animal is off the ranch unless it trains), and their Titan asleep in its den once that is built (`Ranch.den`); `Creatures.asset` names the model for a species or variant.

- Trading (decisions 0084, 0099): two players in one server swap up to `StationTrade.MAX` animals each. The trade lives in `StationsServer` (`trades`), never in a save; any change makes both sides unready and holds Ready; both ready locks the window for a timed Confirm. On both confirmed the server checks `StationTrade.commit` on copies of both saves, writes both sides to `TradeLedger`, checks again and applies both with no yield between. `commit` is idempotent by trade id (the save's `trades.log`), and `healTrades` applies at login any recent ledger side a save lacks: keep both true.
- Every creature model must be passed to `describe` on the server (species, variant, six stats as attributes): the card under the cursor (decision 0039) reads only those, and finds the model by its unseen `Hit` box (tag `CreatureHit`). A model's `Hp` attribute, once fights set it, drives the health bar.

- Fight presentation (decision 0048, display only): `WaveShow` sets `HitPower`, `HitDmg`, `HitAt` on a struck model and `StopAt` / `StopFor` on a melee attacker, and makes archers' arrows and mages' bolts (in the player's show folder); `Creatures.client` turns them into sparks, a shock disc, a flinch and a hit-stop, `ArenaCamera` into a FOV punch. Timings and curves are `src/shared/FightFx.luau`. Read these attributes tolerantly (they arrive in any order) and never let one decide or delay a result.
- Per-species animal moves (decision 0048; SHANNON's picks, STATION-116): each species' idle, attack and power attack, and a variant's own power attack (keyed by its asset), are `src/shared/AnimalMoves.luau`, generated by `tools/blender/stations/moves` from the concepts there (change them there); `Creatures.client` takes them into `ATTACKS` and `MAGICS` and plays an idle like a stance (moves start from it and settle back into it), falling back to `ATTACK` / `MAGIC`; each clip must be 0.95 s (attack) or 1.6 s (power) like `Creatures.LENGTH`. A power attack's magic is its spell (`stations-client/Spells`, by asset then species), which replaces the shared charge and slam: `WaveShow` sets `AimAt` (the ground under the target) and `AimHeight` before it plays; without them the spell lands ahead of the animal. A spell's error is caught and warned once, never stops the animator. Spells wear the Hot core look (SHANNON's pick, 2026-11-22: layered glow, charge rings, a star flash and shock ring on the hit) when their asset is in `Spells.HOT`, which also says where each one's charge gathers; the kit draws the look, so a spell's own code never changes for it. `WaveShow` sets `Anim = "win"` on the winners after the last hit.
- Fight rhythm (decisions 0048, 0097): `WaveShow` marks the fight's last hit `HitFinal`; `Creatures.client` slows every clip after it (`FightFx.slow`, by reading a later time than the server's). The finish is the slow motion; the camera never cuts in (decision 0106: an action camera on the canyon's side pans between the two front lines, and only a power hit nudges it). Perfect, combos and the shield are rules (`BattleRules`), the ring, pops, warnings and bars are display (`Hud`); runs are `WaveRuns`, kept on the server. Power attacks have per-animal clips (`MAGICS`): look up by asset, then species, then the shared clip, so a variant adds its own under its asset (`creature_lion_shadow_forge`).
- UI (decision 0091): build every menu from `Style` (the Savanna surfaces, `Style.sheet` with `Style.tab` in its left column, `Style.toast`, `Style.sound`); never colour a card or button by hand outside `Style.C` and `Style.RARITY`. A shadowed card's or button's colour is on its `Face` child (`Style.paint`, `Style.kind`; a button copies its own `BackgroundColor3` and text properties to `Face` and `Label`), and neither can hold its own list layout (the face and shadow would be laid out): put a frame inside it. The Ranch menu's building pictures are screenshots (`PICTURES`): retake them when a building changes.
- Power attacks (decision 0092): the player's team fires them by tapping (`power` request, `WaveShow.power`). A tap is replayed from the fight's start (`BattleRules.battle` with `taps`), so anything that changes the fight must stay deterministic and must not change what happens before a tap's time.
- Shared fights (decision 0108): a fight is a party's, one to three players (`StationsServer`: `partyOf`). Everyone standing in the wave portal's ring (`World.inPortal`) with a team joins when a wave starts, at a run's first wave and at each one after. One field: while a party fights or picks its boon, another's `wave` is refused (`field`). Every member is paid by their own save as alone and keeps their own pot; a carried player's wave becomes their best (`StationProgress.clear` with `carried`). The run is the party's (`WaveRuns`): its `hp` is every member's animals in the fight's slot order (0 for the knocked out, who fight the next wave at `WIPED_BACK`: `WaveRuns.setup`, alone too), and the boon is voted (`WaveRuns.tally`, settled when all have voted or `VOTE_SECONDS` (7) after it was offered; the HUD counts it down). In the rules an animal's `owner` (1 to 3) is its player's place in the party: bonuses stay inside a player's team, the front line is `BattleRules.front(players)` long, a foe attacks the team it faces and a click strikes the foe nearest its player's team; with one player nothing differs. `WaveShow` keeps a show under its first member (the host) and its models in that player's folder: the others find it by their `FightFolder` attribute, a side-1 model carries `Owner` (a UserId) and `Seat` (which of that player's animals), and `power` takes a seat. Never assume a fight has one player: go through `present(show)` and the model's `Owner`.
- The battlefield (decision 0108): `Battlefield.build` makes it at `World.FIELD`, `Battlefield.REACH` up the road north of the hub (the approach is part of it); the hub's terrain stops where its ground begins (`terrain` in World.luau, kept off the ranches' land) and the portal's stone floor covers the seam; unseen parts tagged `FieldStand` (where the teams stand, looking -Z) and `FieldGate` (the summoning circle's near edge) are all `WaveShow` reads of it, with the `Ground` folder beside the stand to set feet on. Teams stand at `Battlefield.seat`, Titans `TITAN_BACK` behind; the crowd in files `FILE` apart from `CROWD_AT` ahead. `ArenaCamera.client` stands behind the player's own team from `CamFrom`, `CamFocus` and `CamScale`, sways slowly from side to side and looks at the clash with the nearest standing foe (STATION-127); `ArenaMood.client` fades in the field's light up the road north. A fight starts only from the wave portal (World.luau), but anyone may walk up and watch.
- The waves end (decision 0107): `LevelRules.LAST` (300) is the last, `StationProgress.canFight` refuses any past it, and a run that clears it is banked with no boon offer (`last` on the result). Up to `STEEP_FROM` (100) every stat of a foe grows 3% a wave; after it only health and strength grow (`LevelRules.might`, more on every wave) and the rest hold, so a foe never swings faster than at wave 100. A run's boons stack without limit by SHANNON's choice: the steep waves are what ends a run, so re-run the run simulation of decision 0107 after any change to boons, clicks or the curve.
- Swarm waves (decision 0101): `LevelRules.enemies` builds a wave's crowd (`CROWDS`: rushes, minions first, elites, the boss last) and scales it to `LevelRules.classic`'s power x the row's `power`; `LevelRules.power` is the wave's power in the menus. The factors were set so test fights end as against the classic waves: re-check them (as decision 0101 says) after any change to the crowd rules in `BattleRules` (front line, rushes, splash, crowd power attacks). `WaveShow` stands foes in rows of `BattleRules.FRONT`, runs later rushes in at their `arrivals` and steps a foe into the front line at its `engaged` time, both from the latest replay.
- The pit fight (decision 0106): in a run's fight (`Setup.clicks`, set by `WaveRuns.setup`) the team's own attacks are `CLICK_SLOW` times slower (1 since 2026-11-24: clicking is a bonus) and clicks add strikes. The HUD's click layer (a ScreenGui under the HUD) answers a click at once and sends batches (`strike`, half a token); `WaveShow.strike` lays them from `TAP_AHEAD`; `BattleRules` strikes with the next standing animal in turn (a tap on slot `STRIKE`, log entries marked `click`, drawn lighter through `HitClick`). Each animal's power attack has a quick press (`BattleRules.pressOf`, judged by `BattleRules.perfect`): the HUD draws the ring, the needle, the chain's marks and the mash's count, and `power` carries a mash's presses, capped on the server's clock. The press is the animal's class's (decision 0109, `PRESS_OF_ROLE`: attack ring, defense needle, utility chain, Titan mash), so a new species needs nothing. The ring is held while it fills (`HOLD_TENTHS`; `power`'s count is tenths held, capped by the server's clock); `PressPops.card` draws every pop-up. The pop-ups stand beside their team, each on its animal's side, with no line to it (SHANNON, 2026-11-24), gone the moment the press is done (a refused press is re-sent for a few seconds, and only then is a fresh card shown); a press messed up or left `AUTO_FIRE` seconds fails (`FAILED`, `EXPIRE_FAILS`: a plain hit marked `fail`; the run simulation presses instead); clicks count only from the player's `FightFrom` (server time, set when the wave starts, not during the walk-in); another player's full meter shows only as a marker (`stations-client/PressPops.luau`, which the HUD hands each card). Stages (`LevelRules.stage`) are display only: `WaveShow` sets the player's `FightWave`; the HUD's stage strip reads it.
