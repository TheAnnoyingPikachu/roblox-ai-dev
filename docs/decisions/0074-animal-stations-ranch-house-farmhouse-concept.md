# 0074: Animal Stations: the Ranch House as a classic farmhouse (concept)

Status: proposed (a concept for SHANNON; nothing is in the game).
Date: 2026-11-17. Task STATION-069.

## What SHANNON asked

"also make a new concept for the ranch house. make it a classic looking wood and stone farm house".

## What was made (`docs/concepts/ranch-house-card.png`)

A farmhouse of fieldstone and timber, in three looks for the house's levels, as the house has now.

| Look | Levels | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| The cottage | 0, 1 | timber boards on a fieldstone footing, a gabled shingle roof, a stone chimney up its end, a small roofed stoop, a woodpile and a rain barrel |
| The farmhouse | 2, 3 | a fieldstone ground floor, a timber half floor under the roof with two dormers, a porch the length of its front with a rail and a rocking chair, lamps at the steps |
| The great farmhouse | 4 | two floors, fieldstone below and timber above, a cross wing with its gable to the front, a porch as far as the wing, two chimneys, a cupola with a weather vane, flower boxes |

All three have lit windows with pale frames and green shutters, a red door, and the house's name
over the way in. The card shows the great farmhouse large, the three looks side by side, then the
back, the porch and the gable end with its chimney.

- Fact: every look fits the place the house has on the homestead (the widest is 43 studs across
  with its chimneys and 27 deep; the place allows 56 and 40).
- Assumption: three looks, at the same levels as the house in the game now.

## For SHANNON to decide

1. The look: go, or changes.
2. The go-ahead to build the three as models and upload them in place of the present three
   (`home_house_1` to `3`). The present ones would then be unused uploads; their decals can be
   archived.

## Not done

- Nothing is built for the game, exported or uploaded. Not tried in the game engine or on a phone.
- The inside is not modelled: the door opens the ranch menu, as now.
