# 0028: Animal Stations Health stat, Mana power attacks, hilltop ranches

Status: proposed (for SHANNON at the first Animal Stations checkpoint)
Date: 2026-11-13. Builds on 0026 and 0027.

## Decision

- **Health is a fifth trainable stat.** HP in battle = 20 + 1.5 x Health (it no longer comes from
  the total of all stats). Every species has a Health value; Titans have the most.
- A fifth station, the **Healing Spring**, trains Health. It sits at the end of the south walk.
- **Mana sets how often power attacks happen.** Each turn an animal's meter gains 10 + Mana; at
  200 the turn is a power attack (damage and heal x 2) and the 200 is spent. A Mana 8 Warthog
  powers up about every 11 turns, a Mana 90 Mandrill every 2.
- Attack still comes from the animal's best stat other than Health; Speed sets the turn order.
- **Ranches are far out, each on its own hill** (400 studs from the plaza, 22 up), reached by a
  long road and a ramp, so the hub has room to grow. Still one ranch per road (north, east, west).
- The Animals panel shows HP and has one train button per stat.

## Level reach after this change (draft)

Starters 8, starters trained in every stat 23, rares 23, legendary team 36, trained rares +
Titan 44.

## Not decided yet
