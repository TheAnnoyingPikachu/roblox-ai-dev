# 0063: Animal Stations: the hub's centre as a place to gather (concept)

Status: proposed (a concept for SHANNON; the centre of decision 0060 stays in the game until this is
decided). Date: 2026-11-16. Task STATION-055.

## What SHANNON asked

"after this can you make a new concept for the center, less shrine like and more communal area like".

## What was made (`docs/concepts/hub-commons-card.png`)

The same plaza (48 studs to its kerb) and the same four roads, so it fits where the built centre
stands. Nothing to look up at: no great egg on a lotus, no ring of statues, no shrines, no
gateways. Things to sit at, stand round and walk between instead.

- In the middle, a fire pit ringed by two rows of curved benches with cushions, the back row a
  step higher, open towards each road.
- South-west, the market: five striped stalls with crates and barrels, and the egg seller's cart,
  where the Golden Egg now sits on straw among smaller eggs.
- North-west, the cafe: an open hut with a counter and an awning, four round tables under parasols.
- North-east, the stage: a roofed platform with a painted back and lamps along its front, three
  rows of benches before it.
- South-east, the green: a big meeting tree with a bench round its trunk, a swing and lanterns in
  its boughs, a pond with a pier, a water trough and a rail for the animals, picnic tables, hay.
- Strings of flags from a ring of poles out to the lamps on the kerb; a finger post at each road
  (WAVE ARENA, RANCHES, WAY IN), a notice board, planters, and a plain timber arch over the south
  walk with the game's name.
- Warm cobbles in rings, with a round of pale flags and a band of brick round the fire.

Animals stand in it at their real size, as visitors: a lion by the fire, a rhino at the trough, an
elephant coming in from the arena road.

## For SHANNON to decide

1. This centre, the built one (0060), or parts of both. Recommendation: none; it is a matter of
   taste. Parts mix easily, since both are built from pieces on the same plaza.
2. Whether any of it should do something. Fact: everything on the plaza today is scenery. A stage,
   a notice board and stalls suggest uses (events, news, vendors) that are not designed.

## If SHANNON approves

Not started. It would be built like 0060: pieces as models through the upload workflow, unseen
walls for what is solid. The twelve models of the built centre would be unused, apart from any
piece kept.

## Not done

- No night view: the fire and the strings of lights would matter most then, and the hub is always
  in daylight.
- Not measured: 99,000 triangles as rendered, with its seven animals and the land round it.
