# 0133: Animals rigged by the pipeline play baked clips

Date: 2026-11-28. Status: accepted (SHANNON: "go ahead and do those first three on the rhino and then 8 and 6 1 and
3 and 10"; 10 was "the game playing baked clips on the new bones"). Tasks: STATION-198, STATION-199.

## Context

Facts:

- A skinned animal (decision 0102) has seven bones: body, head, four legs, a tail. Its clips are pose functions in
  `Creatures.client`, written once for every animal and scaled by leg length. A leg turns whole about its hip, so
  feet hover or sink and the body is lifted to make up for it.
- SHANNON's notes on the Eternals this week were all about that rig: a walk that "looks quite janky", "a lot of
  stretching going on", models "generated posed not an A frame".
- The rig pipeline (`tools/blender/stations/rigs`) gives a generated model 22 bones (root, hips, spine, chest, neck,
  head, a thigh, shin and foot to each leg, four tail bones) with IK legs, and measures its landmarks itself. On the
  Crystal Rhino its worst clip stretches 4.4 % of the surface; on the seven-bone rig the Sahara Mammoth's blended
  legs stretched 7.3 % until they were cut free of its body (STATION-197).

## Decision

1. An animal made by the pipeline keeps **all its bones** in the game. The server and the spells still find `body`
   (the hips), `head`, `leg_fl` to `leg_br` (the thighs) and `tail` (its first bone) by name.
2. Its clips are **keyed in Blender and baked**, bone by bone, into `src/stations-client/Clips/<asset>.luau`
   (generated by `rigs/export_game.py`). `Clips.pose` plays them: one clip each for idle, walk, attack, magic, hit,
   die and win, eased between frames, 0.12 s to change from one to the next. They are not uploaded as Roblox
   animations: the game animates on each client from the model's attributes (`Anim`, `AnimAt`), with hit-stops and
   slow motion read off the same clock, and that stays as it is.
3. A baked turn is written in the Root's axes about the bone's own joint. The client turns it into the bone's axes
   with the rest frame it measures on the model, so an importer's choice of bone axes cannot break a clip.
4. A walk is played one cycle for each `stride` of ground the server moves the animal (`Creatures.speed`), so planted
   feet do not slide whatever its size.
5. Every other animal is posed as before. An asset is a pipeline animal exactly when `Clips` has a module of its name.

## Consequences

- A species gets its own clip set (`rigs/clipsets/<species>.py`) and a variant its own power attack beside it, to go
  with its spell. Lengths and blow times stay the game's (0.95 s and 0.44, 1.6 s and 0.92).
- About 57 KB of client script for an animal's seven clips.
- Shortcut: the training clips (heave, swim, study, soak, brace) and the Titan's sleep show the idle on a pipeline
  animal. They need clips of their own before a pipeline animal is seen training much.
- The first is the Crystal Rhino (STATION-199). The rest of the animals follow once SHANNON has seen it in the game.
