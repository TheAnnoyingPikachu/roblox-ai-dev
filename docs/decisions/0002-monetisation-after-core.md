# 0002: Monetisation after the core loop

Date: 2026-10-24
Status: Accepted by SHANNON (decision card)

## Decision

Bake Rush ships free first. Optional cosmetic-only monetisation comes after the core loop passes playtests and a Roblox policy review. Paid items never change progression, coins or tier speed.

## Why

The blueprint adds monetisation only after the core game works. The audience is under 18, so the policy review must come first.

## Consequences

- The v0.1 spec's "no paid items" stands until a later spec change adds cosmetics.
- Monetisation becomes its own task and needs its own review, as the blueprint requires.
