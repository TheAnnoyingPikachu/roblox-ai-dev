# 0031: Animal Stations: no legendary-only egg

Status: proposed (for SHANNON at the first Animal Stations checkpoint)
Date: 2026-11-13. Replaces the Legendary Egg of 0029.

## Decision

- There is **no egg that hatches only legendaries**. Instead, one egg **unlocks at player level
  15 and can drop legendaries** among its other animals.
- For now that is the Golden Egg (50,000 coins): the Lion 50%, the three uncommons 15% each, and
  the Titan Elephant (the one legendary species) 5%. Draft odds.
- **The eggs will be overhauled soon** (from SHANNON), so the four eggs, their names, prices and
  odds are placeholders. Do not build on them.
