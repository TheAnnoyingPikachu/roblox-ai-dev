# 0082: Animal Stations: gentle giraffe concepts

Status: proposed (concepts for SHANNON; the game's giraffes are unchanged). Date: 2026-11-17. Task STATION-082.

## What SHANNON asked

"can you redo me some giraffe concepts, dont make them scary looking or anything just make them look good".

## What was made

The same faceted giraffe as the game's (decision 0076) with a gentle head, in three colourings
(`tools/blender/stations/assets/giraffes.py`, `gentle`; the assets `concept_giraffe_*` are cards only):

- What changed from the game's giraffe: no heavy brow; big round eyes with a brown iris, a shine and
  three lashes; a pink blush on each cheek; soft round furry tufts on its horns instead of sharp dark
  tips; a short fluffy mane of tufts instead of spikes; rounder patches; a light muzzle.
- `docs/concepts/giraffe-sunny-card.png`: golden, warm orange patches, a russet mane.
- `docs/concepts/giraffe-savanna-card.png`: cream, dark chocolate patches.
- `docs/concepts/giraffe-gem-card.png`: a gentle Diamond Giraffe: pale blue, gem patches, a glowing gem
  on each horn tip, blue eyes.

- Assumption: this is a change of look only; the Giraffe's numbers and eggs (decision 0079) stay.
- Note: decision 0033 says animals look dangerous, not cute; these go the other way, as SHANNON asked.

## For SHANNON to decide

1. Which look becomes the Giraffe (or changes to one), and whether the gem one becomes the Diamond
   Giraffe.
2. The go-ahead to rebuild and upload them in place of the game's two giraffe models (and their
   rainbow coats).
