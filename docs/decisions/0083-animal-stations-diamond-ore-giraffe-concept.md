## What SHANNON asked

"make it meaner looking again and make it look like a diamond ore, like the entire body" (after the
gentle concepts of decision 0082).

## What was made (`docs/concepts/diamond-ore-giraffe-card.png`)

The Diamond Giraffe with the mean head of the game's giraffe (decision 0076: a heavy brow, glowing slit
eyes, sharp glowing diamond horns, a crest of glowing crystal down the neck), its whole body made of
grey stone in blocks with cracks between them (the "rock" surface on every piece of hide), set with
diamond ore: small cyan gem chips all over, and about seventy diamonds breaking out of the body, the
neck and the legs (most painted gem, a third glowing), with the clusters on its shoulders and back.
Built by `ORE` and `ore_extra` in `tools/blender/stations/assets/giraffes.py` (the asset
`concept_giraffe_diamond_ore`, a card only).

- Assumption: it takes the Diamond Giraffe's place (mythic, twist defence; decision 0079); the regular
  Giraffe stays as in the game.
